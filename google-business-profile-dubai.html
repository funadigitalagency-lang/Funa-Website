When someone searches "marketing agency Dubai" or "best dentist near me", Google shows a Map Pack — three local businesses displayed prominently at the top of results, above all websites. Most Dubai businesses have incomplete, unoptimized profiles. That's your opportunity.

Complete Setup Guide

01

Claim or Create Your Profile

Go to business.google.com. Search for your business — Google may have automatically created a listing. Claim it, or create a new one. Verify via phone call or postcard to your Dubai address.

02

Complete Every Single Field

Business name, category (primary + secondary), address, phone, website, hours, description. Google rewards completeness — incomplete profiles rank lower. No exceptions.

03

Write a Keyword-Rich Description

750 characters to describe your business. Include your primary services and Dubai location naturally. Example: "Funa Digital Marketing is Dubai's leading growth marketing agency, specializing in lead generation, Meta Ads, and social media management for UAE businesses."

04

Add All Your Services

Google lets you list individual services with descriptions. Each service is an opportunity to rank for specific search terms. Add every service you offer, even minor ones.

05

Upload 20+ High-Quality Photos

Office, team, products, and work samples. Then add 5-10 more per month. The 520% call increase stat is real — photos are your #1 conversion lever on your profile.

Advanced Optimization Strategies

  • NAP Consistency: Name, Address, and Phone must be identical everywhere online — website, social, directories. Inconsistency kills your local ranking.
  • Choose the Right Primary Category: The most important ranking factor. Research which category your top-ranking competitors use. "Digital Marketing Agency" ranks differently than "Marketing Consultant".
  • Enable Messaging: Turn on Google messaging so clients can WhatsApp directly from your profile. Respond within 1 hour.
  • Pre-populate Q&A: Add the questions your clients commonly ask. Controls the narrative and improves your comprehensiveness score.

Getting 5-Star Reviews Systematically

"Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor. More reviews = higher map position = more clients."

  • Create your direct review link from the dashboard — copy it and use it everywhere
  • Send immediately after delivering great work or receiving a compliment
  • Simple ask: "Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Takes 30 seconds." + link
  • Respond to every single review — positive and negative, within 24 hours
  • Never ask for reviews in bulk or offer incentives — Google penalizes this

Using Google Posts to Stay Active

  • Post weekly: offers, events, new services, blog articles, team news
  • Posts with photos get 5x more views than text-only
  • Use clear CTAs: "Book Now", "Learn More", "Call Now", "Get Offer"
  • Posts expire after 7 days — weekly consistency matters for ranking signals
