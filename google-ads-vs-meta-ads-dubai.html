Google Ads vs
Meta Ads in Dubai:
Which One Wins?

The most common question we get from Dubai businesses: 'Should I run Google Ads or Facebook/Instagram Ads?' The honest answer depends entirely on your business type, goals, and budget. Here's how to decide.

The Fundamental Difference

Google Ads captures demand. People are actively searching for what you offer. You appear at the exact moment of intent.

Meta Ads creates demand. You interrupt people scrolling through their feed and make them aware of something they might want.

This distinction determines everything. The wrong channel for your business type is guaranteed to waste money — regardless of how well-optimized your campaigns are.

When Google Ads Wins in Dubai

Google Search Ads outperform Meta when:

  • High search volume for your service — 'personal injury lawyer Dubai', 'AC repair Dubai', 'SEO agency UAE'. If people are searching for you, capture them.
  • High ticket, low volume sales — legal services, B2B software, industrial equipment. These buyers research before deciding.
  • Emergency or urgent services — medical clinics, locksmiths, towing. People search when they need help NOW.
  • Local service businesses — Google Maps ads for restaurants, salons, and clinics convert extremely well in Dubai.

When Meta Ads Win in Dubai

Meta outperforms Google when:

  • Visual products — fashion, food, interior design, real estate. Instagram's visual format sells these better than any text ad.
  • Impulse purchases — e-commerce products under AED 500 that people buy based on desire, not research.
  • New category creation — if nobody is searching for your product yet, Google has no search volume to capture. Meta lets you create the demand.
  • Retargeting — Meta's retargeting is the best in the industry. If you have website traffic, this is where you convert them.

The Dubai-Specific Verdict

For most Dubai SMEs with AED 10,000-30,000/month ad budgets, we recommend:

  • 60% Meta Ads — Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns + retargeting
  • 40% Google Ads — Search campaigns for high-intent keywords

This split works because it covers both stages of the buying journey: Google captures people ready to buy, Meta converts people who are interested but not yet searching.

Cost Comparison in the UAE Market

Average CPCs (Cost Per Click) in Dubai 2026:

  • Google Search (competitive industries): AED 8-25 per click
  • Google Search (less competitive): AED 2-8 per click
  • Meta Feed Ads: AED 1-4 per click
  • Meta Story Ads: AED 0.5-2 per click
  • LinkedIn (B2B): AED 15-45 per click

Raw click cost doesn't tell the full story — what matters is cost per qualified lead. A AED 20 Google click that converts at 10% is cheaper than a AED 2 Meta click that converts at 0.5%.
