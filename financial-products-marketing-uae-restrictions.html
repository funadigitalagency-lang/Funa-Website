Financial Products Marketing in UAE: Navigating Ad Restrictions on Meta, Google and TikTok

The platform-by-platform compliance and creative guide for prop trading and crypto brands in UAE — what is allowed, what gets ads rejected, and how to scale without account bans.

F
Funa Marketing Team

Every prop trading firm and crypto company in UAE eventually runs into the same problem: their ads get rejected, their account gets flagged, or their campaigns get paused without warning. Platform policies around financial services — and crypto and trading specifically — are among the most complex and frequently updated in digital advertising. What worked six months ago may not work today.

This guide maps the current policy landscape on each major platform, explains what triggers rejection, and gives you the creative and structural framework for running compliant campaigns at scale without losing accounts.

The fundamental rule: platform financial ad policies restrict claims, not categories. You can advertise prop trading and crypto in UAE. What you cannot do is promise returns, project profits, or imply guaranteed outcomes. Understand this distinction and most compliance problems disappear.

PLATFORM BY PLATFORM — WHAT WORKS IN 2026

META (FACEBOOK + INSTAGRAM)
⚠ WORKS WITH PRIOR AUTHORIZATION

Meta permits financial services advertising including prop trading and crypto with prior written authorization. Apply through Meta's financial services ad authorization process. Approval typically takes 5–15 business days. Without authorization, financial product ads will be rejected or run with severely limited reach.

  • Apply for authorization before creating any financial campaigns — without it you are building on sand
  • Creative must not include: guaranteed returns, specific profit projections, "risk-free" language
  • Permitted: challenge structure explanation, payout examples with clear "past results" disclaimers, regulatory status
  • Landing pages are reviewed — claims on landing pages must match ad claims and include risk disclaimers
  • Never run financial ads from new ad accounts — build account history with non-financial content first
  • Multiple ad accounts at different warm ages reduce single-account risk if one gets suspended
GOOGLE ADS
✓ WORKS WITH CERTIFICATION

Google requires certification for crypto exchange and wallet advertising. Apply through Google's cryptocurrency exchange certification program. Once approved, search and display ads for crypto are permitted in UAE with specific disclosure requirements. For prop trading (not crypto), standard Google financial services policy applies — no certification required, but income claim restrictions still apply.

  • Apply for crypto certification if you operate an exchange — certification is account-level, not campaign-level
  • Prop trading firms: standard Google Ads financial services policy applies. No special certification required
  • Search ads for prop trading: "funded trader account", "prop firm challenge", "trading capital" — all permissible
  • Avoid: "become rich trading", guaranteed income claims, exaggerated earnings projections
  • Display URL must match domain of advertised product — no redirect tricks
TIKTOK
⚠ PAID ADS VERY RESTRICTED

TikTok's financial services advertising policy is the most restrictive of the major platforms. Direct crypto exchange advertising and direct prop trading challenge promotion are not permitted through standard paid TikTok ads without specific authorization — which is rarely granted to non-institutional brands in UAE. The strategy here is organic content and influencer partnerships, not paid direct response.

  • Paid direct-response ads for crypto or prop trading: avoid — high rejection rate and account risk
  • Organic brand content: fully permitted — educational, market commentary, community content
  • Influencer whitelisting: creator posts to their own account, brand runs as whitelisted ads — lower policy risk
  • If you have explicit TikTok financial services authorization: standard financial claims restrictions apply
SNAPCHAT
✓ UNDERUSED — LOWER FRICTION

Snapchat's financial services policy has less friction than Meta or TikTok for educational and community-focused financial content. For UAE audiences — particularly UAE nationals and GCC residents — Snapchat's 93% penetration rate makes it a high-value underutilized channel. Financial services authorization required but approval process is generally faster and less complex than Meta's.

  • Apply for financial services authorization through Snapchat's ad portal before launching
  • Educational financial content performs well — "what is a prop firm challenge" style format
  • Arabic-language creative significantly outperforms English-only for UAE national target audience
  • App install campaigns with financial services apps — if you have a mobile product, Snapchat drives installs efficiently

THE FIVE CREATIVE RULES THAT PREVENT REJECTIONS

❌ Language That Gets Ads Rejected
  • "Earn $10,000 per month trading"
  • "Guaranteed profits from your first challenge"
  • "Risk-free trading capital"
  • "Make a living from trading in 30 days"
  • "Bitcoin will reach $200K — invest now"
  • "Passive income from crypto with no effort"
  • Any specific return projections without extensive disclaimers
✓ Language That Passes Review
  • "Trade our capital — pass the challenge to get funded"
  • "Challenge fee from $149 — account sizes up to $200K"
  • "12,400 traders funded since 2023"
  • "90% profit split on verified funded accounts"
  • "VARA-licensed crypto exchange — UAE regulated"
  • "Buy, sell, and hold crypto — instant AED deposits"
  • Past performance with explicit disclaimer: "Past results do not guarantee future returns"
