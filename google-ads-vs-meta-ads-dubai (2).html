Google Ads vs Meta Ads:
Which One Wins
in Dubai?

The most common question from Dubai businesses: 'Should I run Google Ads or Meta Ads?' The answer depends entirely on your business type, goals, and budget. Here's how to decide.

The Fundamental Difference

"Google captures demand. Meta creates demand. The wrong choice for your business type wastes 100% of your budget."

Google Ads captures demand. People are actively searching for what you offer. You appear at the exact moment of intent. If nobody is searching for your service, Google has no volume to capture.

Meta Ads creates demand. You interrupt people scrolling their feed and make them aware of something they might want. Even people who weren't looking for you can become customers.

High
Intent captured by Google — people are actively searching to buy
Broad

When Google Ads Wins in Dubai

  • High search volume for your service — "personal injury lawyer Dubai", "AC repair Dubai", "SEO agency UAE"
  • High ticket, low volume sales — legal services, B2B software, industrial equipment
  • Emergency or urgent services — medical clinics, locksmiths, towing
  • Local service businesses — Google Maps ads for restaurants, salons, and clinics

When Meta Ads Win in Dubai

  • Visual products — fashion, food, interior design, real estate
  • Impulse purchases — e-commerce products under AED 500
  • New category creation — if nobody is searching for your product yet
  • Retargeting — Meta's retargeting is the best in the industry for converting warm audiences
