Email & WhatsApp Sequences for Prop Firms

From first click to challenge purchase — the exact automation sequences, subject lines, and WhatsApp flows that convert interested traders into paying customers.

F
Funa Marketing Team
Prop Trading Specialists · Dubai
May 2026
12 min read

Most prop firms have a lead capture problem disguised as a conversion problem. They collect emails from ad clicks, landing page visits, and blog readers — then send those leads a generic welcome email and a promo blast two weeks later. That is not a sequence. That is a missed opportunity worth tens of thousands in monthly revenue.

The average trader who shows interest in a prop firm challenge takes 7–14 days to purchase. They are comparing options, reading reviews, watching YouTube. A well-designed automation sequence stays relevant throughout that window — answering objections, building trust, and presenting the right offer at the right moment.

The channel reality in 2026: Email open rates in prop trading average 22–28%. WhatsApp open rates average 85–95%. Run both in parallel — email for education and long-form content, WhatsApp for direct offers and time-sensitive nudges.

SEQUENCE 1 — THE LEAD MAGNET NURTURE

Triggered when a trader downloads a resource (challenge guide, trading rules PDF, comparison sheet) from your website. They have shown intent but have not seen pricing yet.

LEAD MAGNET SEQUENCE — 7 DAYS
Avg Open Rate34%
Conversion6–9%
0
Instant
Subject: Your [resource name] + one thing most traders miss
Deliver the lead magnet immediately. Add one genuinely useful insight they did not ask for. First impression sets the tone — be helpful, not salesy. No CTA in this email.
Goal: Deliver value, establish credibility
1
Day 1
Subject: The #1 reason traders fail Phase 1 (not what you think)
Educational content about a common challenge mistake. Position your firm's rules as easier to navigate than the industry average. Soft mention of your challenge — no hard sell. Link to blog article.
Goal: Position your firm as the clearer, fairer option
3
Day 3
Subject: [Name] passed our $200K challenge in 22 days. Here's how.
Real funded trader story with specific details — account size, days to pass, payout amount, strategy used. Human proof. Link to full video testimonial. First explicit mention of starting a challenge.
Goal: Social proof + first direct CTA
5
Day 5
Subject: What is actually different about [Your Firm] (honest answer)
Honest comparison. What makes your firm different from FTMO, Apex, MyForexFunds. Be specific — rules, payout speed, support quality. Acknowledge what others do better if relevant. Builds trust through honesty.
Goal: Differentiation + overcome comparison objections
7
Day 7
Subject: Still thinking about it? Here's 10% off your first challenge
Discount offer with 48-hour expiry. Only for leads who have not purchased. This is the conversion email — direct, clear, time-limited. Single CTA: "Start My Challenge." No other content.
Goal: Conversion with urgency

SEQUENCE 2 — THE ABANDONED CHALLENGE PAGE

Triggered when a trader visits the challenge pricing page but does not purchase. This is your highest-intent lead segment — they were close.

ABANDONED PAGE SEQUENCE — 5 DAYS

1 Hour
Subject: Still deciding? Here's the answer to the most common question
Address the most common objection. Usually: "How fast is the payout?" or "What if I fail?" Answer it directly. Link back to challenge page. No discount yet — let trust do the work first.
Goal: Remove the number 1 objection
24
24 Hours
Subject: 3 traders started their challenge yesterday. Here's what they said.
FOMO plus social proof. Show that others are moving forward. Recent signup notifications (anonymized with country), community activity. Creates urgency without false scarcity.
Goal: Social momentum + FOMO
3
Day 3
Subject: Your challenge account is reserved for 24 more hours
Urgency mechanism. Pair with a limited-time discount (10–15%). Single button CTA. Short email — 5 sentences maximum.
Goal: Urgency + conversion offer
5
Day 5
Subject: Last chance — discount expires tonight
Final email. Hard deadline on the offer. One sentence explaining what they will miss. One CTA. If they do not convert after this, move to long-term re-engagement sequence.
Goal: Final conversion push

WHATSAPP — THE HIGH-CONVERSION CHANNEL

WhatsApp Business API (through providers like WATI, Interakt, or Respond.io) lets prop firms automate WhatsApp messages with opt-in contacts. Open rates of 85–95% make it the highest-performing conversion channel available in this market. The conversation-style format gets 4–6x higher response rates than email for the same content.
