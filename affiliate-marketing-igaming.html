Why Affiliates Dominate iGaming Player Acquisition

Affiliates take on all the marketing risk — you only pay when they deliver a depositing player. It's the most efficient acquisition model in iGaming.

The iGaming affiliate model is uniquely powerful: affiliates invest their own time and money to rank review sites, run paid traffic, and build YouTube channels — all sending qualified, conversion-ready players to your casino. You pay only when a player deposits and plays.

Commission Models Explained

Revenue Share (RevShare)

The affiliate earns a percentage (typically 25-45%) of net gaming revenue from players they refer — forever. This model attracts the highest-quality affiliates who focus on sending players with high lifetime value. The best affiliates prefer RevShare because their earnings grow over time as their referred players keep playing.

Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

A flat fee (typically $100-$400) for each depositing player the affiliate sends. CPAs attract affiliates who prioritize volume over quality. Risk: affiliates may send lower-quality, bonus-hunting players who churn quickly.

Hybrid Deals

A combination: a smaller CPA ($50-$150) plus a lower RevShare (15-25%). This balances cash flow for the affiliate while maintaining your interest in player quality. The most common deal structure for established operators.

Sub-Affiliate Commissions

Super-affiliates who recruit other affiliates earn an additional 2-5% of their sub-affiliates' revenue. This creates a viral recruitment effect in your affiliate program.

How to Recruit Top Affiliates

The top 10% of affiliates generate 80% of affiliate revenue. Recruiting them requires a compelling offer and personal outreach.

01

Identify the Top Affiliates in Your Target Markets

Search for your target keywords ("best casino UK", "top sportsbook Canada") and note which affiliate sites rank on page 1. These are the affiliates you want. Google rankings = player delivery capability.

02

Create a Compelling Affiliate Offer

Top affiliates work with dozens of casinos. Your offer needs to stand out: competitive RevShare (30%+), no negative carryover, fast payment (weekly or bi-weekly), and dedicated affiliate manager access.

03

Personal Outreach Over Email Blasts

The best affiliates receive hundreds of generic partnership requests. Personalized outreach that shows you've reviewed their site and explains specifically why your casino is a good fit for their audience converts 5-10x better.

04

Attend iGaming Conferences

SiGMA, ICE, Affiliate World — the top affiliates attend these events. Face-to-face relationships convert to better deals, faster onboarding, and more prominent placement on their sites.

05

Offer Exclusive Bonuses

Give top affiliates exclusive bonus codes to offer their audience — better welcome offers than what's publicly available. This gives their readers a reason to click their link rather than going directly to your site.

Managing Your Affiliate Program for Maximum Performance

  • Dedicated affiliate managers: Assign a specific person to manage relationships with your top 20 affiliates. Personal relationships drive better placement and promotion.
  • Monthly performance reviews: Share traffic and conversion data with affiliates — they can optimize their promotions when they understand what converts.
  • Creative asset updates: Provide fresh banners, landing pages, and bonus offers regularly. Stale creative kills conversion rates.
  • Fast and reliable payments: The fastest way to lose top affiliates is late payments. Pay on time, every time.
  • Transparent reporting: Give affiliates real-time access to their stats — clicks, registrations, deposits, revenue. Opacity breeds distrust.

Affiliate Fraud Prevention

⚠️ Fraud is a Real Risk

Affiliate fraud costs iGaming operators millions annually. Common schemes include: bonus abuse (players depositing only to claim bonuses then withdrawing), multi-accounting, fake registrations, and traffic manipulation. Implement fraud detection tools, monitor player LTV closely, and include clawback clauses in affiliate agreements for fraudulent traffic.
