Building Your Ecommerce Foundation

Platform Selection for UAE

  • Shopify: Best overall for UAE ecommerce. Easy setup, great payment integrations, Arabic support.
  • Salla: Built specifically for Arab ecommerce. Native Arabic, excellent Gulf payment integrations.
  • Zid: Another GCC-focused platform with strong UAE market presence.

UAE Payment Methods You Must Accept

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa + Mastercard) — non-negotiable
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay — extremely high adoption in UAE
  • Tabby / Tamara (Buy Now Pay Later) — increases average order value 30-40%
  • Cash on Delivery — still preferred by 35% of UAE shoppers for first orders

Driving Traffic That Converts

01

Meta Ads — Your Primary Growth Channel

Instagram and Facebook shopping ads are the #1 customer acquisition channel for UAE ecommerce. Use Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns for stores with 50+ purchase conversions. Minimum AED 5,000/month budget.

02

TikTok Shop and TikTok Ads

TikTok's in-app shopping is gaining massive traction in UAE. Fashion, beauty, food, and lifestyle products sell exceptionally well. Cost per purchase is 40-50% lower than Meta for trending products.

03

Google Shopping Ads

For products people actively search for. Set up Google Merchant Center, upload your product feed, and run Performance Max campaigns targeting UAE.

04

Influencer Partnerships

UAE micro-influencers with genuinely engaged audiences consistently deliver 3-5x ROAS for ecommerce brands. Always negotiate usage rights so you can run their content as paid ads.

Conversion Rate Optimization for UAE Ecommerce

Average ecommerce conversion rates in UAE: 1.5-3% for well-optimized stores. Here's how to get there:

  • Premium product photography: Multiple angles, lifestyle shots, and video are non-negotiable for conversion in UAE's visual-first market.
  • Arabic language option: Adding Arabic increases conversion by 25-40% for Arabic-speaking audiences.
  • Trust signals: Display UAE trade license number, physical address, and phone number prominently.
  • Fast delivery: Same-day or next-day in Dubai is now an expectation. Free delivery above AED 150-200 threshold.
  • Clear returns policy: UAE consumers abandon carts when return policies are unclear or difficult.

💡 The Growth Formula

Traffic × Conversion Rate × Average Order Value × Repeat Purchase Rate = Revenue. Most UAE ecommerce businesses obsess over traffic and ignore the other three. Optimizing all four simultaneously is what separates AED 50K/month stores from AED 500K/month stores.
